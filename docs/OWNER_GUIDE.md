# Owner guide

Crowned by Chrissy is set up for one stylist. Use the dashboard from your phone.

## Bottom navigation

- **Today** — upcoming appointments, requests, deposits waiting, exceptions
- **Requests** — approve, suggest another time (require acceptance), or decline
- **Calendar** — agenda plus day/week filters, phone/WhatsApp bookings, blocked time
- **More** — services, gallery, clients, payments, notifications, settings

## Approving a request

A request does not hold the calendar. When you approve:

1. Confirm service, time, duration, buffer, price, and deposit
2. If you changed the time or quoted a new amount, tick **Require client to accept**
3. Approval places a temporary hold until the deposit is paid (unless you approve without a deposit)

If deposits are on but Cash N’ Go is not configured, the app will not invent a payment link.

## Payments

Deposits are credited toward the service total. Record cash/offline payments from Payments — they are never labelled CNG-verified.

If money arrives after a hold expires, it appears as an exception for you to resolve.

## Gallery

Upload photos or videos, set captions, category, linked service, and crop focal point. Videos need a poster and captions file when you have them. Empty video sections stay hidden on the public site.

## Clients

Private notes never go out in customer messages. Retained deposits show as credit and can be applied once to a replacement appointment.
